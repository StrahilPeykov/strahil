Across two internship periods—December 2023 to July 2024 and March to August
2025—I worked on a Mendix application intended to replace fragmented,
spreadsheet-driven management of outsourced workforce resources.

I started without prior Mendix experience and learned the platform while
establishing the initial application structure and relational domain model. The
work covered structured imports, validation, searchable resource views,
workflows, role-aware behaviour, dashboards, reporting, and technical and
functional documentation.

## Working with a larger team

During the second period, three more interns joined. As the person with the most
context, I helped them get started with the domain model and project
conventions, split up features, and coordinated changes that touched the same
parts of the application.

## Recovering lost work

Several developers initially worked too directly against the main line. A bad
integration broke the application and forced us to reconstruct lost changes. I
helped pause and recover the work, then moved the team toward branch-based
development. We also started writing down what a feature was meant to do before
merging it and coordinating changes more carefully.
